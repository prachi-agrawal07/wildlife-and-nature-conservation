WHO WE ARE

About Nature Conservation

Nature conservation means protecting the environment, wildlife and natural resources so that future generations can enjoy a healthy and balanced planet.

🌳

Forest Protection

Forests provide oxygen, shelter and food. Protecting them helps preserve biodiversity.

🐅

Wildlife Protection

Wildlife conservation protects animals and plants from habitat loss, poaching and extinction.

🌎

Environmental Care

Reducing pollution and using resources wisely helps maintain a healthy environment.

WHY IT MATTERS

Importance of Conservation

All living organisms are connected. Protecting nature helps maintain the balance of life.

🌱 Biodiversity

Conservation protects the variety of living organisms and keeps ecosystems healthy.

💧 Natural Resources

It helps preserve clean water, fertile soil, forests and other essential resources.

🌦️ Climate Balance

Forests and oceans absorb carbon and help regulate the Earth's climate.

🐾 Future Generations

Protecting nature today helps future generations inherit a healthier planet.

DISCOVER THE WILD

Our Beautiful Wildlife

Every species has a role in nature. Here are some remarkable animals that need protection.

Bengal Tiger

Tigers help maintain the balance of forest ecosystems as apex predators.

Elephant

Elephants disperse seeds and help shape the environments in which they live.

Wild Birds

Birds contribute to seed dispersal, pollination and natural pest control.

UNDERSTAND THE CHALLENGE

Major Threats to Nature

Human activities and environmental changes can damage habitats and threaten biodiversity.

🪓

Deforestation

Cutting down forests destroys animal habitats and reduces biodiversity.

🏭

Pollution

Plastic, chemicals and waste contaminate air, water and soil.

🌡️

Climate Change

Rising temperatures and changing weather can disrupt natural ecosystems.

🚫

Illegal Wildlife Trade

Illegal hunting and trading threaten many wild animal populations.
